📖 The Present Simple Tense

When do we use it?

• Habits and routines: I wake up at 7 a.m.

• Facts and general truths: The sun rises in the east.

• Feelings and states: She loves chocolate.

• Schedules: The train leaves at 9 p.m.

Structure — Affirmative

SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / Theybase formI play tennis.
He / She / Itbase + s / esShe plays tennis.

👉 Add -es after verbs ending in -o, -s, -x, -ch, -sh: go → goes, watch → watches

Structure — Negative

don't + base verb (I, you, we, they)

doesn't + base verb (he, she, it)

I don't like coffee. / He doesn't like coffee.

Structure — Questions

Do + subject + base verb?

Does + subject + base verb?

Do you live here? / Does she work here?
